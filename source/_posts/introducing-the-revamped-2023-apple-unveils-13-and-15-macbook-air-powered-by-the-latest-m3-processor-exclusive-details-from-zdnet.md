The Pro chip model features the M3 Pro chip, which is a step up from the first level M3 chip. It starts at 12 main processing cores (six performance and six efficiency) and 18 graphics cores. If you regularly work on graphics-intensive tasks, this would be the chip for you.

In terms of specs, this MacBook Pro M3 Pro includes an 11-core CPU, a 14-core GPU, 18GB unified memory, and 512GB of SSD storage.
